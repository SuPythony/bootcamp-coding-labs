    Part 2 copy constructor and copy assignment. Which of the two refactors
    did you choose (helper method, or copy-and-swap), and how does it
    eliminate that duplication? If you chose copy-and-swap, also explain
    why it is exception-safe.

    YOUR ANSWER:
    These were duplicated:

    for (int i = 0; i < _size; i++) {
        _friends[i] = user._friends[i];
    }

    Also copying the private variables is also duplicated but in copy 
    constructor they are in member-initializer list.

    I used the copy-and-swap idiom. It eleminates duplication as the
    actual copying part is done by just using the copy constructor.
    Then swap to get those copied members to the original object.
    We have to make it exception-safe as otherwise if an exception
    occurs during swapping, the objects might be left in a broken,
    unspecified state.
